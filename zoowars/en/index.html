Overview

About The Game

Send animals out from your zoo and take down your opponent's zoo!

Zoo Wars is a real-time competitive tower defense game where cute animals and powerful fruit cards clash in lively battles.

Build a deck from six animal and fruit cards, deploy units in real time, and race to destroy your opponent's zoo first.

In Friend Battles, you can even play against friends who do not own the game. Invite them with a URL or invite link, and they can join instantly from a browser with no installation required.

Online PvP With CPU Fallback

Play Friend Battles with people you know or ranked matches against players around the world. Depending on matchmaking conditions, a CPU opponent tuned to the player's strength may step in.

Battle Friends Who Do Not Own The Game

Friend Battles let your opponent join even if they do not have the game. Share an invite link or URL, and they can jump into the battle from a browser without installing anything.

Streaming And Video Posts Welcome

Gameplay streams, videos, and posts are welcome. Monetization, including ad revenue and Super Chats, is allowed for both individuals and organizations.

Animals Full Of Personality

Mice, rabbits, monkeys, elephants, giraffes, and more join the battle with adorable looks and unique abilities.

Fruit Cards That Turn The Tide

Use apples for precise attacks, melons to sweep the ground, watermelons to blast an entire zoo, and more satisfying effects.

Simple Controls, Deep Strategy

No complicated controls required. Anyone can start playing quickly, while deck building and tactical timing create plenty of strategic depth.

Cross-Platform Play

Steam and mobile players can battle together, so you can enjoy zoo battles anytime, anywhere.
